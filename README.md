## HR Analytics AI Dashboard
# Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce patterns using SQL and Power BI. AI-based visuals such as Key Influencers and Decomposition Tree are used to identify major attrition drivers.

# Tools Used

- SQL (Data Cleaning & Transformation)

- Power BI (Dashboard & AI Analysis)

- DAX (Measures & Calculations)

- Dashboard Pages

1. HR Executive Overview

Total Employees

Attrition Rate

Average Salary

Smart Narrative AI Summary

2. AI-Based Attrition Drivers

Key Influencers (AI)

Attrition by Job Role

Salary Band Analysis

Tenure Impact

3. Workforce Demographics

Education Distribution

Salary by Department

Job Satisfaction Analysis

4. Retention Risk & Strategy

Overtime Impact

Promotion Delay Analysis

High Risk Employees Table

# Key Insights

- Overtime significantly increases attrition risk

- Low salary band employees show higher turnover

- Sales department has highest attrition
